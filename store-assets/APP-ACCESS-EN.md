# Google Play App access — Alnoor School 2.0.4

The app uses role-based school accounts. No registration, payment, OTP, or external identity provider is required for the reviewer test accounts.

## Quick access (recommended)
1. Open the app.
2. On the Arabic sign-in screen, scroll to “دخول تجريبي سريع للمعاينة”.
3. Tap one of the role buttons:
   - “دخول كمشرف” — Administrator
   - “دخول كمعلم” — Teacher
   - “دخول كطالب” — Student
   - “دخول كولي أمر” — Parent

These accounts use synthetic demonstration school records. Do not enter real student information.

## Reviewer access maintenance
Keep all four accounts enabled and reusable throughout review. Do not rotate their passwords or require a one-time code while the review is pending. The quick-access panel may be removed from the public production experience after the review only when equivalent reusable reviewer credentials remain available in Play Console and the demonstration accounts stay isolated from real school records.
